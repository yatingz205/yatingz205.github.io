Yating Zou

Department of Biostatistics, UNC Gillings School of Global Public Health.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Biostatistics at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, advised by Michael R. Kosorok, and a graduate research assistant at the UNC Gillings Center for Artificial Intelligence and Public Health (CAIPH).


My research develops nonparametric methodology at the interface of distributional estimation, kernel methods, and reinforcement learning, with a focus on supporting sequential decision processes for real-world systems in structured environments. Structure here can come from dependent, missing, hierarchical data, as well as multi- and hierarchical agents.
